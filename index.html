1. Information Paperkeeper Processes

Paperkeeper is designed to process documents and receipts locally on your device.

Depending on the features you use, Paperkeeper may process:

  • Images of documents and receipts
  • PDF files and other documents that you choose to import
  • Text contained in documents
  • Information extracted from documents and receipts
  • Information necessary to operate the application

Documents and receipts

When you scan a document or receipt using the camera, or import a document through the application, Paperkeeper processes that content to provide its scanning, organization, OCR, and information-extraction features.

The documents you process may contain personal or sensitive information. Paperkeeper does not intentionally upload your documents to a remote server as part of its normal document-processing functionality.

Extracted information

Paperkeeper uses on-device optical character recognition (OCR) and an on-device artificial intelligence model to extract information from documents and receipts.

Depending on the document, this may include:

  • Merchant or company names
  • Dates
  • Prices and amounts
  • Currency
  • Invoice or receipt numbers
  • Product or item information
  • Other text contained in the document

The AI processing is performed locally on your device after the required AI model has been downloaded.

2. AI Model Download

Paperkeeper requires an internet connection when the AI model is not yet available on your device.

During initial setup, Paperkeeper downloads the required AI model from a GitHub Release.

Important: The AI model is downloaded to your device so that AI processing can subsequently be performed locally. Paperkeeper does not send your documents or receipts to GitHub as part of the AI processing performed by the application.

The GitHub service may process technical information associated with the download request, such as your IP address and other information normally associated with accessing a website or downloading a file. Such processing is governed by GitHub's own privacy practices.

You can review GitHub's privacy practices here: GitHub Privacy Statement.

After the AI model has been downloaded, Paperkeeper's core document recognition and AI extraction features are designed to work without an internet connection.

3. Camera Access

Paperkeeper requests access to your device's camera so that you can scan documents and receipts.

Camera access is used only when you use the scanning functionality.

Paperkeeper does not continuously access your camera or record from your camera in the background.

4. Document and File Import

Paperkeeper allows you to choose documents and files from your device for processing.

Files that you choose to import are processed locally by Paperkeeper.

Paperkeeper does not intentionally upload imported documents to a remote server for processing.

5. How Information Is Used

Information processed by Paperkeeper is used to:

  • Scan documents and receipts
  • Recognize text in scanned documents
  • Extract structured information from documents
  • Organize and display documents
  • Store documents and extracted information locally
  • Provide reminders or notifications when enabled
  • Provide the application's functionality

Paperkeeper does not use the contents of your documents for advertising.

6. Local AI Processing

Paperkeeper is designed so that its AI-based document processing takes place locally on your device.

After the AI model has been downloaded, documents and receipts processed by the AI are provided to the locally installed AI model rather than being sent to a cloud-based AI service.

Paperkeeper does not intentionally upload your documents or extracted information to a remote AI service for processing.

7. Data Storage

Paperkeeper stores documents, extracted information, and application data locally on your device as necessary to provide its functionality.

Paperkeeper does not require you to create an account or maintain a cloud account for its core functionality.

Because information is stored locally, you are responsible for protecting your device and maintaining backups of information that you consider important.

Deleting Paperkeeper or clearing its application data may delete locally stored information.

8. Data Sharing

Paperkeeper does not sell, rent, or share your documents or extracted document information with advertisers or data brokers.

Paperkeeper's core document-processing functionality does not require documents to be uploaded to a remote server.

If you explicitly use an Android or device feature to share, export, or send a document to another application or service, the information may be transferred to that application or service. That transfer is initiated by you and is subject to the recipient's own privacy policy.

9. Internet Connections

Paperkeeper requires an internet connection when the AI model needs to be downloaded from GitHub.

After the model has been downloaded, the application's core scanning, OCR, document processing, and AI extraction features are designed to operate offline.

Paperkeeper does not intentionally transmit the contents of your documents to our servers.

The GitHub service hosting the AI model may receive technical information necessary to deliver the download, such as your IP address and standard connection information.

10. Notifications

Paperkeeper may use local notifications when you enable notification-related features.

These notifications are generated by the application and are used to provide reminders or other functionality requested by you.

Paperkeeper does not use notifications for advertising.

11. Third-Party Software and Services

Paperkeeper uses third-party open-source libraries and software components to provide features such as:

  • Document scanning
  • OCR and text recognition
  • PDF processing
  • Local database storage
  • Local AI/LLM processing
  • File handling
  • Notifications
  • Image processing

These libraries are integrated into the application to provide its functionality.

The inclusion of a software library does not mean that Paperkeeper sends your documents to the developer of that library.

The AI model download requires a connection to GitHub because the model is distributed through a GitHub Release.

12. Advertising and Analytics

Paperkeeper does not intentionally use your documents or extracted information for targeted advertising.

Paperkeeper does not include advertising as part of its core functionality.

Paperkeeper does not intentionally collect analytics about the contents of your documents.

13. Children's Privacy

Paperkeeper is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

Paperkeeper does not require users to create an online account, and its core document processing takes place locally on the device.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to us through an online feature, please contact us using the contact information below.

14. Data Security

Paperkeeper is designed to minimize transmission of document information by processing documents locally on your device.

However, no software or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should use appropriate security measures on your device, such as a device passcode or biometric authentication, to help protect information stored by Paperkeeper.

15. Data Retention and Deletion

Paperkeeper does not maintain a central account containing your documents or extracted document information as part of its core functionality.

Information stored by Paperkeeper remains on your device until you delete it using the application's available deletion features, remove the application, or otherwise remove the relevant local application data.

Information that you explicitly export or share to another application or service may remain with that application or service according to its own policies.

16. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights concerning your personal information, including rights to access, delete, correct, or restrict the processing of personal information.

Because Paperkeeper's core document information is stored locally on your device rather than in a central user account, many actions concerning that information can be performed directly by you through the application's controls or by deleting the relevant local data.

If you have questions about your privacy rights or Paperkeeper's privacy practices, you can contact us using the contact information below.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we make changes, we will update the "Effective date" at the beginning of this Privacy Policy.

If a future version of Paperkeeper introduces new data collection, online services, advertising, analytics, or other material changes to how information is processed, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
